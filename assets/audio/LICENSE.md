# Original demo instrumental

`superdoc-instrumental.wav` is generated from the original composition in
`scripts/compose-roi-music.py`. It uses synthesized tones and deterministic noise,
with no third-party recordings, samples, melodies, or artist imitation.

The composition, synthesis source, and resulting instrumental are dedicated to
the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
No attribution is required. The release credits it as “Original instrumental · CC0.”

The separately generated spoken narration uses OpenAI’s standard Cedar voice;
it is identified in the video as AI-generated narration. This music dedication
does not relicense SuperDoc’s logo or any other brand assets.
