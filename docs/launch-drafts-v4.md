## LinkedIn

A model decision is the start. The developer still needs to turn it into a Word edit someone can review.

Our SuperDoc × Jev demo now walks through that whole path: review an agreement, approve language, inspect real redlines, then download Word. Clause replacements, order-form cells and a numbered safeguard share the document with counsel's existing revisions.

The result shows verified Word operations, active processing time and estimated model cost. Human reading time is excluded. Every operation has a target, receipt and readback check.

You can also freeze one set of clause evidence and compare Jev with two pinned OpenAI models. Every response, failure and disagreement is visible. A small fictional evaluation and its frozen expected labels are included in the repo. This is developer infrastructure, not a claim of legal accuracy.
